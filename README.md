# PALMONAS — AI-Powered Visual Marketing Case Study

> A strategic visual marketing audit and AI-generated brand asset portfolio for PALMONAS jewellery.

## What It Does

This project conducts a competitive visual marketing analysis of PALMONAS against India's leading jewellery brands (Tanishq, Malabar Gold & Diamonds, Kalyan Jewellers, CaratLane) and delivers two AI-generated visual campaign concepts that address identified brand gaps — a "light festive look" and an "everyday office wear" collection.

## The Problem

PALMONAS positions itself as "luxury made affordable for modern youth" — a demi-fine jewellery brand for daily wear. However, their visual marketing lacked emotional storytelling, lifestyle context, and cinematic campaign imagery compared to established competitors. This project bridges that gap using Generative AI to create proof-of-concept brand visuals.

## What Was Built

- **Competitive Audit**: Mapped PALMONAS against 4 major Indian jewellery brands on campaign imagery, lifestyle storytelling, and visual identity
- **Gap Analysis**: Identified deficiencies in emotional storytelling, office/brunch lifestyle contexts, and layered coordinated looks
- **AI-Generated Concepts**: Created two full visual campaign concepts using GenAI image models with iterative prompt refinement for brand alignment
- **Brand Strategy Recommendations**: Structured feedback on how PALMONAS can transition from product-shot-first to emotion-first visual marketing

## Key Outcomes

- Demonstrated a scalable, AI-first approach to visual marketing collateral creation
- Delivered two production-ready visual concepts aligned to PALMONAS' "luxury made affordable" positioning
- Showcased ability to convert strategic marketing objectives into precise AI image prompts
- Proof-of-concept for moving brand visuals from static product photography to depth, texture, and emotional moment-driven imagery
